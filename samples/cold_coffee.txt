Title: Cold Coffee
Author: ShotByShot test script

INT. CORNER CAFE - MORNING

Rain on the windows. A half-empty cafe. NORA (30s, composed) sits at a table by the window, two mugs in front of her. One is untouched.

BEN (30s, rumpled, soaked) hurries in and drops into the chair across from her.

BEN
Sorry. Train.

NORA
I ordered for you. It's cold now.

Ben wraps his hands around the mug anyway.

BEN
You said we needed to talk.

NORA
(quietly)
I took the job. In Lisbon.

A long beat. Ben sets the mug down.

BEN
When?

NORA
Three weeks.

BEN
And us?

Nora slides a small envelope across the table.

CLOSE ON the envelope. A house key inside.

NORA
I think you already know.

The BARISTA (20s) arrives with a coffee pot.

BARISTA
Top up?

BEN
No. Thanks.

The barista retreats. Ben stares at the key.

BEN
Was it ever going to be enough?

NORA
It was enough. It just wasn't everything.

Ben stands, leaves the key on the table, and walks out into the rain.

EXT. CORNER CAFE - CONTINUOUS

Ben stops on the pavement. Through the window, Nora hasn't moved.

He turns up his collar and walks away.

CUT TO:

INT. CORNER CAFE - LATER

The rain has stopped. Nora sits alone, holding Ben's cold mug.
